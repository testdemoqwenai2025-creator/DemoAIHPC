Physics · mechanics

Classical mechanics, rigid-body dynamics, Lagrangian/Hamiltonian.

lagrangian(T, V)

Lagrangian mechanics — L = T − V (kinetic energy minus potential energy).

▾
L = T − V    d/dt(∂L/∂q̇) − ∂L/∂q = 0

The Lagrangian L = T − V encodes the dynamics. The Euler-Lagrange equation gives the equations of motion from L.
