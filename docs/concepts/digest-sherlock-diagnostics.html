SHERLOCK at-home: Cas13a-based multiplexed CRISPR diagnostics with paper-strip readout

Gootenberg, J. et al. (Broad Institute + McGovern) · Nature · 2024-12-19

Abstract

The Broad/McGovern team reports the field-deployable SHERLOCKv2 platform — Cas13a + Cas12a collateral cleavage with lateral-flow paper-strip readout, detecting 4 viral targets in a single 30-minute reaction at room temperature.

Clinical sensitivity/specificity on 248 patient samples (SARS-CoV-2, influenza A/B, RSV): 96.4% / 98.1% vs RT-qPCR gold standard. Limit of detection: 10 copies/μL — comparable to RT-qPCR.

The platform has been deployed in 14 low-resource settings (sub-Saharan Africa, rural India). The full oligo sequences + Cas variants are released under CC-BY-NC 4.0.

Design Cas13a crRNA guides for SHERLOCK: pick 28-nt spacers in conserved regions of the target genome. Avoid off-target matches in the human transcriptome.

Math → Code derivation

crRNA = 28-nt spacer + 24-nt direct repeat (DR) → Cas13a binds DR, spacer targets viral RNA
Cas13a crRNA = 28-nt spacer (viral-target-specific) + 24-nt direct repeat (Cas13a scaffold). After target binding, Cas13a's collateral activity cleaves nearby reporter RNA — producing a fluorescent or paper-strip signal.
Step 1: Identify conserved regions
conserved = windows where Shannon entropy < 0.5 (across 1000+ genomes)
Slide a 28-nt window across the target viral genome; identify windows with low Shannon entropy (high conservation across strain diversity). These are robust detection targets.
Step 2: Spacer extraction
spacer = 28-nt subsequence of conserved region
Each conserved window yields a 28-nt spacer candidate. The spacer is the part of the crRNA that hybridises to the viral RNA target.
Step 3: Off-target check
off_target = matches in human transcriptome (BLAST, E-value < 1)
Check each spacer against the human transcriptome. Reject any with significant off-target matches (would cause false positives from host RNA cleavage).
Step 4: Direct-repeat append
crRNA = spacer + 5'-GATTTTCTACTAACGATAGTGTC-3' (24-nt DR for LwaCas13a)
Append the species-specific direct repeat (DR) for the chosen Cas13a ortholog. LwaCas13a (Leptotrichia wadei) uses a 24-nt DR — the most clinically validated ortholog.
Step 5: Multiplexing
multiplex = mix 4 crRNAs (one per target) + 4 distinct reporters (one per crRNA)
SHERLOCKv2 multiplexes 4 crRNAs (e.g. SARS-CoV-2 N, influenza A NP, influenza B NS1, RSV F) with 4 distinct reporters — 4 targets in a single 30-min reaction. Paper-strip readout: each reporter produces a band at a different lateral-flow position.
