Gemini 2.5 medical: 1T-param multimodal model with native 3D medical imaging

Google DeepMind Health Sciences · Google Health blog · 2026-12-12

Abstract

Gemini 2.5 medical is a 1T-parameter (Mixture-of-Experts) multimodal LLM fine-tuned for clinical use. The 2026 medical update adds native 3D medical imaging support (CT, MRI volumes processed directly — no 2D slicing required) + a longitudinal patient-timeline understanding module.

On the MultiMedQA-Pro benchmark (500 multi-turn clinical conversations), Gemini 2.5 medical scores 0.91 — beating AMIE (0.85), Mistral Med-Large (0.84), and GPT-5B-clinician (0.78). On 3D-imaging tasks (tumour segmentation, organ detection), it matches specialist models (nnU-Net 0.87 vs Gemini 0.86 Dice).

Released via Google Cloud Healthcare API. HIPAA-compliant. Used in 23 NHS trusts for GP-to-specialist referral triage.

Step 1: Case parse
case = parse(clinical_text); volume = parse_dicom_3d(dicom)
Parse the clinical case text (symptoms, history, labs) + the 3D DICOM volume directly (no 2D slicing). Gemini 2.5 is the first LLM to process 3D medical volumes natively.
Step 2: 3D DICOM encode
vol_emb = 3D_ViT(volume, patches=(8,8,8))
A 3D Vision Transformer encodes the DICOM volume as 8x8x8 patches. Each patch is 32x32x32 voxels. Produces a 4096-token sequence.
Step 3: Longitudinal timeline
timeline = PatientTimeline(case.history, case.labs, case.imaging_history)
The timeline module organises all patient events chronologically. Gemini 2.5 attends to temporal patterns (e.g. 'creatinine rising over 3 months').
Step 4: Multi-turn reasoning
analysis = Gemini25Med(case_emb + vol_emb + timeline)
Gemini 2.5 medical produces a multi-turn reasoning chain: differential diagnosis → evidence for/against → next-step workup. MultiMedQA-Pro: 0.91 (vs AMIE 0.85).
Step 5: Care plan
plan = reason(analysis); plan includes: diagnosis, meds, follow-up, red-flags
The care plan includes: working diagnosis, recommended medications (with dosing), follow-up interval, and red-flag symptoms to watch for. Clinician-acceptance: 89%.
