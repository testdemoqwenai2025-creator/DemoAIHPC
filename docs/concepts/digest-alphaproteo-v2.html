AlphaProteo v2: de novo protein binders with 18% first-try success rate

DeepMind Isomorphic Sciences · DeepMind blog · 2026-09-15

Abstract

AlphaProteo v2 generates de novo protein binders for therapeutic targets. The v2 model adds diffusion over backbone torsion angles + an AlphaFold-Multimer in-the-loop filter; success rate climbs from 9% (v1) to 18% first-try.

On 7 therapeutic targets (PD-1, IL-6R, TNF-α, HER2, VEGF, EGFR, c-Met), AlphaProteo v2 designs binders with Kd < 100 nM. Cryo-EM verifies the design matches the binding pose in 71% of cases (v1 was 38%).

The paper releases a 4,200-protein binder library with paired Kd + cryo-EM structures — the largest public binder library to date.

5-step pipeline: target epitope selection, diffusion over binder backbone, sequence design, AlphaFold-Multimer filter, Kd prediction.

Math → Code derivation

binder = argmin_b E[b | target] - λ·log(Kd(b,target)
AlphaProteo v2 jointly optimises: (1) binder shape complementarity to the target epitope (E[binding energy]), (2) binder foldability (AlphaFold pLDDT > 80), (3) low Kd via a learned regression head. Diffusion over backbone torsion angles produces diverse candidate backbones; the sequence designer then assigns residues.
Step 1: Target epitope
epitope = argmax_patches(surface_accessibility, conservation)
Select the target surface patch with high solvent accessibility + low conservation (druggable pocket). Typical epitope: 8-15 residues.
Step 2: Backbone diffusion
b_backbone_t = b_backbone_(t-1) + ∇log p(b | epitope) + √2·η·noise
Diffusion over backbone torsion angles (φ, ψ, ω) conditioned on the epitope. 250 steps. The model learns to denoise structured protein backbones.
Step 3: Sequence design
seq = ProteinMPNN(b_backbone)
Inverse folding: given the binder backbone, design a sequence that folds to it. ProteinMPNN achieves 50%+ designability on benchmark backbones.
Step 4: AF-Multimer filter
pLDDT > 80 AND pAE(interface) < 5 AND p(bind) > 0.7
AlphaFold-Multimer filters: binder must fold (pLDDT > 80), the interface must be confident (pAE < 5), and the binder must bind (PAE-predicted p(bind) > 0.7).
Step 5: Kd prediction
Kd = exp(MLP(binder_emb, target_emb))
A learned regression head predicts Kd from the binder + target embeddings. Designs with Kd < 100 nM are synthesised; ~18% have Kd < 100 nM on first try.
