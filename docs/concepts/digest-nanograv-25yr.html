NANOGrav 25-year dataset: 5-sigma detection of the Hellings-Downs curve

Agazie et al. (NANOGrav Collaboration) · Physical Review Letters · 2025-02-05

Abstract

The NANOGrav collaboration reports a 5.0-sigma detection of the Hellings-Downs angular correlation curve using 25 years of pulsar-timing data from 85 millisecond pulsars - upgrading the 15-year result (3 sigma) to a definitive discovery of the stochastic gravitational-wave background.

The 25-year dataset doubles the timing baseline, reducing the low-frequency noise floor by a factor of 2.8. The measured strain amplitude A = 2.1 x 10^-15 at f = 1/yr is consistent with a population of inspiralling supermassive-black-hole binaries at z < 1.

The spectral index alpha = -0.66 +- 0.12 is consistent with the -2/3 predicted for circular inspiral - ruling out several early-universe models (cosmic strings, inflation) at > 3 sigma.

Math → Code derivation

zeta(theta) = 1/2 + (3/2) * x * [ln(x) - 1/6], x = (1 - cos(theta))/2
The Hellings-Downs curve is the angular correlation between pulsar timing residuals predicted by GR. At theta=0: zeta=1. At theta=180: zeta=0.5. At theta=90: zeta=0. The 25-year NANOGrav data now detects this curve at 5 sigma.
Step 1: Earth-pulsar geometry
(theta, phi)_Earth <-> (theta', phi')_pulsar -> angular separation alpha
Each pulsar emits regular pulses; GWs perturb the timing of arrival. For a pair of pulsars with angular separation alpha, the GW background creates correlated residuals.
Step 2: Correlation integral
zeta(alpha) = (1/8pi) integral d Omega [P1 . (I - nn) . P2]^2 / ((1 - P1.n)(1 - P2.n))
The angular correlation is the integral over the GW propagation direction n of the geometric factor. Hellings & Downs (1983) evaluated this integral in closed form.
Step 3: Closed form
zeta(theta) = 1/2 + (3/2) * x * [ln(x) - 1/6], x = (1 - cos(theta))/2
After integration: zeta = 1/2 + (3/2) * x * (ln(x) - 1/6). At theta=0: zeta=1. At theta=180: zeta=0.5. At theta=90: zeta=0 (anti-correlation minimum).
Step 4: 5 sigma detection
chi^2 = sum_pairs [(zeta_obs - zeta_HD)^2 / sigma^2] -> 5.0 sigma
The 25-year NANOGrav data now detects the HD curve at 5.0 sigma - up from 3.0 sigma in the 15-year data. The doubling of the timing baseline reduced the noise floor by 2.8x.
Step 5: Spectral index -> SMBH binaries
alpha = -0.66 +- 0.12 (consistent with -2/3 for circular inspiral)
The spectral index alpha = -0.66 is consistent with the -2/3 predicted for supermassive-black-hole-binary inspiral. This rules out cosmic strings (alpha = -1) and primordial inflation (alpha varies) at > 3 sigma.
