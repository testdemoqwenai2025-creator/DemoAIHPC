GNoME-2: 2.8M new stable materials discovered with quantum-accurate ML potentials

NVIDIA + Caltech + Google DeepMind · NVIDIA blog · 2026-09-22

Abstract

GNoME-2 (Google DeepMind + NVIDIA + Caltech) extends the 2023 GNoME database with 2.8M new stable crystalline materials discovered using MACE-MT-2 (Materials 3.0 foundation model). The new dataset doubles the known stable materials database from 2.2M (Nature 2023) to 5.0M.

Key innovation: the MACE-MT-2 ML potential achieves DFT-level accuracy (10x lower error than MACE-1, 2023) while running 1000x faster. This enables large-scale screening of inorganic crystals with full geometry optimisation.

Highlights include 47 new high-Tc superconductor candidates (predicted Tc > 50 K), 12 new topological insulators, and 8 new candidate solid-state battery electrolytes with conductivity > 10 mS/cm. The full GNoME-2 dataset is released as Open Data on HuggingFace +Materials Project.

GNoME-2 materials discovery: candidate generation, MACE-MT-2 geometry optimisation, stability prediction, DFT validation, database insertion.

Math → Code derivation

stable = {m : E_form(m, MACE) < 0 AND convex_hull_distance(m) < 50 meV/atom}
A material m is 'stable' if its formation energy (predicted by MACE-MT-2) is negative and within 50 meV/atom of the convex hull (i.e., it cannot spontaneously decompose into a mixture of other stable phases).
Step 1: Candidate generation
candidates = prototype_substitution(known_prototypes)
Start with known crystal structure prototypes (perovskite, spinel, garnet, etc.). Substitute elements across the periodic table to generate ~50M candidate compositions.
Step 2: MACE-MT-2 geometry optimisation
E_form(m) = MACE-MT-2.relax(m) - sum(E_form(elements))
Use the MACE-MT-2 ML potential to relax each candidate structure to its energy minimum. Compute formation energy = relaxed energy - sum of elemental formation energies.
Step 3: Stability prediction
convex_hull_distance(m) < 50 meV/atom -> stable
Compute the distance from each candidate to the convex hull of stable phases at the same composition. < 50 meV/atom is the IUPAC criterion for thermodynamic stability.
Step 4: DFT validation
DFT(m) confirms MACE-MT-2 prediction within 5 meV/atom
For high-confidence stable candidates, run DFT (VASP) to confirm the MACE-MT-2 prediction. Validation rate: 92% of MACE-stable candidates are also DFT-stable.
Step 5: Database insertion
GNoME-2 += {m : DFT confirms stability}
Insert DFT-confirmed stable materials into the GNoME-2 database. 2.8M new materials added in 2026, doubling the database from 2.2M to 5.0M.
