Janssen AI antibiotic discovery: first new Gram-negative class in 30 years

Janssen Research + MIT · Nature · 2026-10-08

Abstract

Janssen + MIT announce the first new structural class of Gram-negative antibiotics since 1987 — discovered by an AI screening 12M virtual molecules against E. coli + K. pneumoniae. The lead compound, JNJ-7701, has MIC < 0.5 µg/mL against carbapenem-resistant strains.

The AI pipeline: (1) Chemprop MPNN trained on 230k known antibacterials, (2) virtual screening of 12M ZINC + Enamine molecules, (3) docking against 6 essential Gram-negative targets, (4) ADMET filter, (5) synthesis + in-vitro testing. 23 of the top 100 AI-ranked molecules were active; 4 had novel scaffolds.

JNJ-7701 has a novel oxazolidinone-fused-β-lactam scaffold — distinct from all known classes (penicillins, cephalosporins, carbapenems, monobactams). Phase 1 trials planned for Q1 2027.

AI antibiotic discovery: Chemprop MPNN scoring + docking + ADMET filter + synthesis + in-vitro validation.

Math → Code derivation

hit = {m : MPNN(m) > 0.7 AND docking(m, target) < -7 kcal/mol AND ADMET(m) == PASS}
Each candidate molecule m passes if: (a) the MPNN predicts antibacterial activity > 0.7, (b) docking against an essential Gram-negative target gives binding energy < -7 kcal/mol, (c) ADMET properties pass (no tox, no hERG block, logP < 5).
Step 1: Train MPNN
MPNN = train(230k known antibacterials + 1.2M decoys)
Train a Chemprop MPNN on 230k known antibacterials (positive) + 1.2M random ZINC molecules (negative). The MPNN learns to score molecules by predicted antibacterial activity.
Step 2: Virtual screen
candidates = top_N(score(MPNN, 12M ZINC+Enamine), N=100k)
Score 12M virtual molecules from ZINC + Enamine REAL; take top 100k by MPNN score. ~1M molecules per day on a single A100 GPU.
Step 3: Docking
hits = {m : docking(m, target) < -7 kcal/mol for 6 essential targets}
Dock each candidate against 6 essential Gram-negative targets (DNA gyrase, LpxC, MurA, FabI, DHFR, RNAP). Hits with binding energy < -7 kcal/mol against any target proceed.
Step 4: ADMET filter
ADMET(m) == PASS: logP < 5, no hERG, no tox alerts, MW < 600
Filter by ADMET properties: logP < 5 (Lipinski), no hERG channel block (cardiotoxicity), no PAINS/tox alerts, molecular weight < 600 (Gram-negative permeability).
Step 5: Synthesis + in-vitro
MIC(m, E.coli) < 4 µg/mL -> hit; 23/100 hits; 4 novel scaffolds
Synthesise the top 100 AI-ranked hits. Test MIC against E. coli + K. pneumoniae. 23/100 had MIC < 4 µg/mL. 4 had novel scaffolds (no known class match). JNJ-7701 was the lead.
