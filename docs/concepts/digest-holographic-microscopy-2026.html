Holographic microscopy reconstruction via physics-informed diffusion

HHMI Janelia + Princeton · Nature Methods · 2026-11-18

Abstract

A physics-informed diffusion model reconstructs 3D microscopy volumes from 2D holograms (label-free, fast, low-phototoxic). The model embeds the wave equation as a differentiable forward operator, enabling unsupervised training on raw holograms without paired ground-truth volumes.

On 4 modalities (DIC, phase-contrast, holographic, light-field), the model achieves SSIM 0.87-0.94 vs ground-truth confocal volumes, with 100x lower phototoxicity (no scanning required).

Released as HoloScope-AI on PyPI + as a napari plugin. The pretrained models cover 9 cell lines (HeLa, HEK293, U2OS, organoids, neurons) and 5 imaging modalities.

Demo — holographic_reconstruction(hologram, physics_diffusion) - 5-step 3D reconstruction

5-step pipeline: hologram parse, wave-equation forward, diffusion reverse, SSIM check, 3D render.

Math → Code derivation

V_3D = argmin_V ||F(V) - H||² + λ · KL( V | diffusion_prior )
Reconstruct the 3D volume V from the 2D hologram H by minimising: (a) wave-equation forward operator F(V) matches H (physics), (b) V is close to a diffusion-model prior (learned). No paired ground-truth needed.
Step 1: Hologram parse
H(x,y) = |object(x,y, z) ⊗ point_spread|²
The 2D hologram H is the magnitude-squared of the diffracted object wave convolved with the optical point-spread function. Capture: 1 ms exposure, no scanning.
Step 2: Wave-equation forward
F(V) = |FFT(V) · P(k_x, k_y)|²
The forward operator F maps a 3D volume V to a 2D hologram via: take FFT of V, multiply by propagation kernel P, take magnitude-squared. This is the wave equation in the angular-spectrum formulation.
Step 3: Physics-informed diffusion
V_t-1 = V_t - ε (∇_V ||F(V_t) - H||² + λ ∇_V KL(V_t | prior)) + σ η
Reverse-diffusion step that minimises both the physics loss (F(V) close to H) and the KL to the learned prior. 50 diffusion steps; trained on raw holograms only (no ground-truth volumes).
Step 4: SSIM check
SSIM(V_recon, V_confocal) > 0.87 for test set
Validate on held-out confocal-paired volumes: SSIM 0.87-0.94 across 5 modalities + 9 cell lines. Lower phototoxicity: 100x less light exposure vs confocal.
Step 5: 3D render
render(V_3D) via volume ray-casting + alpha-blending
Render the reconstructed 3D volume using GPU ray-casting. Browser-side: WebGL via napari. Inference time: ~3 seconds on a single A100.
