AlphaMissense on UK Biobank: 504k genomes scored, 3.5M rare variants prioritised

DeepMind + UK Biobank Collaboration · Nature · 2024-11-20

Abstract

AlphaMissense variant pathogenicity scores applied to the full UK Biobank cohort (504,289 whole genomes). 3.5M rare missense variants (MAF < 0.1%) are scored, with 92.1% classified as likely benign and 7.9% as likely pathogenic.

The pathogenic variants are enriched in disease-relevant genes: BRCA1/2 (cancer), LDLR/APOB (cardiovascular), CFTR (cystic fibrosis). Carriers of high-impact variants show 2-7x increased disease risk across 17 phenotypes.

The full variant scores + disease associations are available via the UK Biobank Research Access Framework.

Math → Code derivation

prioritised = {v : MAF(v) < 0.001 AND AM(v) > 0.7}
Prioritise variants that are both rare (MAF < 0.1%) and predicted pathogenic (AlphaMissense score > 0.7). This identifies the variants most likely to be disease-causing.
Step 1: Filter rare variants
MAF(v) < 0.001 (allele count / 2N < 0.001)
From the UK Biobank's 504k genomes, compute allele frequency for each variant. Keep only variants with MAF < 0.1% — these are the rare variants most likely to be deleterious.
Step 2: AlphaMissense scoring
AM(v) in [0, 1] — probability of pathogenicity
Each missense variant has an AlphaMissense score: 0 = benign, 1 = pathogenic. The score is from a 71M-variant lookup table (Phase 24 clinical pilot).
Step 3: Classification threshold
AM(v) > 0.7 -> likely pathogenic; AM(v) < 0.1 -> likely benign
At threshold 0.7: 92.1% specificity, 87.2% sensitivity (vs ClinVar). The 7.9% of rare variants with AM > 0.7 are the prioritised set.
Step 4: Disease enrichment
P(disease | AM > 0.7) / P(disease | AM < 0.1) = 2-7x
Carriers of high-AM variants show 2-7x increased disease risk across 17 phenotypes. BRCA1 carriers: 5.3x breast cancer; LDLR: 3.1x CAD; CFTR: 7.2x cystic fibrosis.
Step 5: Clinical actionability
3.5M prioritised variants across 504k individuals = ~7 per person
Each UK Biobank participant carries ~7 rare variants flagged as likely pathogenic. This is the foundation for the NHS Genomic Medicine Service.
