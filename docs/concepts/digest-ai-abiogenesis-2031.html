Futuristic Vision (2031): AI-driven abiogenesis - autonomous chemical evolution simulations

AIHPC Editorial Board - extrapolated from AlphaFold, AlphaEvolve, MACE-MT-2 (2024-2029) · AIHPC Futuristic Vision · 2031-Q3 (extrapolated)

Abstract

This is a futuristic vision, not a real publication. We extrapolate from AlphaFold 4 (2026), AlphaEvolve v3 (2028), and MACE-MT-2 (2026) to predict that by Q3 2031, AI will autonomously simulate full chemical evolution pathways from prebiotic soup to self-replicating protocells.

The vision: an AI 'origin-of-life engine' simulates 10^12 chemical reactions over 10^9 simulated years on an exaflop GPU. The engine starts from HCN + H2O + CH4 + NH3 (Miller-Urey mixture), enumerates all possible reaction products, and applies a fitness function (replicability, energy capture, compartmentalisation).

In 6 of 1000 runs, the engine finds a self-replicating protocell with 18-22 residue RNA + lipid vesicle + autocatalytic network. The full simulation trace is downloadable (10 TB per run).

Math → Code derivation

protocell = argmax_p fitness(p) for p in enumerate_reactions(starting_mix, t=10^9 yr)
The AI engine enumerates all possible reaction products from the starting mixture over 10^9 simulated years, scores each product by fitness (replicability, energy capture, compartmentalisation), and detects when a self-replicating protocell emerges.
Step 1: Starting mixture
S_0 = {HCN, H2O, CH4, NH3, H2S, phosphate}
Start with the Miller-Urey 1953 mixture: HCN, water, methane, ammonia, hydrogen sulfide, phosphate. This is the canonical prebiotic chemistry.
Step 2: Reaction enumeration
products_t = enumerate_reactions(S_t, k=10^12 per step)
Use MACE-MT-2 (2026) + reaction templates to enumerate 10^12 possible reactions per time step. Each time step is ~1000 simulated years.
Step 3: Fitness scoring
fitness(p) = α·replicability + β·energy_capture + γ·compartmentalisation
Score each product p by: (a) replicability (can it template its own synthesis?), (b) energy capture (does it use ATP-equivalents?), (c) compartmentalisation (does it form vesicles?).
Step 4: Selection
S_(t+1) = top_k(products_t, k=10^6, by=fitness)
Select the top 10^6 products by fitness; carry forward to the next time step. Total simulated time: 10^9 years (~10^6 time steps).
Step 5: Protocell detection
protocell if fitness(p) > threshold AND is_self_replicating(p)
A product is a 'protocell' if it exceeds the fitness threshold AND is verified self-replicating (RNA world: 18-22 nt RNA + lipid vesicle + autocatalytic network). In 6 of 1000 runs, a protocell emerged.
