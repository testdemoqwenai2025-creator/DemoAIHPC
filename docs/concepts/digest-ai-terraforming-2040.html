Futuristic Vision (2040): AI-driven terraforming — planetary engineering at scale

AIHPC Editorial Board - extrapolated from Earth-2, AlphaEvolve, fusion control (2026-2035) · AIHPC Futuristic Vision · 2040-Q3 (extrapolated)

Abstract

This is a futuristic vision, not a real publication. We extrapolate from Earth-2 climate twin (2026), AlphaEvolve v4 algorithmic discovery (2026), AI fusion control (2028), and the programmable-enzymes vision (2032) to predict that by Q3 2040, AI will autonomously plan + execute terraforming operations for Mars — making it habitable for humans within a single generation.

The vision: a planetary-scale AI system (TerraForma v3, 100T params) runs a 1m-resolution Mars climate twin + an autonomous robotics fleet (10,000 rovers + 500 atmosphere-processors + 50 fusion-powered factories). TerraForma discovers + evolves the optimal terraforming strategy via AlphaEvolve-style evolutionary search over a multi-objective fitness landscape (atmospheric pressure, temperature, O₂ concentration, water availability).

By 2040, the Mars terraforming project has: raised average temperature from -63°C to -10°C, increased atmospheric pressure from 0.6 kPa to 5 kPa (Stillwell limit), and produced 2% O₂ concentration. Estimated completion (human-habitable without pressure suits): 2060.

TerraForma v3 evolves a terraforming strategy by running a Mars climate twin, testing 1M strategy variants per generation via AlphaEvolve-style LLM-guided mutation, and deploying the Pareto-optimal strategy via an autonomous robot fleet.
Step 1: Mars profile
profile = {P_atm: 0.6 kPa, T_avg: -63°C, O2: 0.13%, CO2: 95%, H2O: trace}
Parse the current Mars atmospheric profile: pressure 0.6 kPa (Earth: 101 kPa), average temperature -63°C, O₂ 0.13%, CO₂ 95%, trace water.
Step 2: Climate twin
twin = Earth2_mars(profile, resolution=1m, duration=20yr)
Run a Mars climate twin (Earth-2 derivative) at 1m resolution for 20 simulated years. The twin models atmosphere, cryosphere, regolith, and subsurface ice.
Step 3: Strategy evolution
strategy = TerraForma_v3.evolve(twin, n_variants=1M, objectives=[P_atm, T, O2, H2O])
Evolve a terraforming strategy: which atmosphere-processors to deploy where, which greenhouse gases to produce, which ice deposits to sublime. Multi-objective Pareto front.
Step 4: Robot deployment
execute(strategy, fleet={10k rovers, 500 processors, 50 factories})
Deploy the Pareto-optimal strategy via an autonomous robot fleet: 10,000 rovers for regolith processing, 500 atmosphere-processors for CO₂ → O₂ conversion, 50 fusion-powered factories for super-greenhouse-gas production (SF₆, C₂F₆).
Step 5: Progress monitoring
progress = monitor(twin, fleet, KPIs=[ΔT, ΔP, ΔO2]); re-plan if ΔKPI < threshold
Monitor progress: ΔT = +53°C (to -10°C), ΔP = +4.4 kPa (to 5 kPa), ΔO₂ = +1.87% (to 2%). If progress drops below threshold, re-evolve the strategy with updated twin data. Completion estimate: 2060.
