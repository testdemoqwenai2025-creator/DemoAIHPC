AlphaProteo v3: 31% first-try binder success rate with conformer-aware design

DeepMind Isomorphic Sciences · DeepMind blog · 2026-12-10

Abstract

AlphaProteo v3 is DeepMind's de novo protein binder design model. The v3 release adds a conformer-aware diffusion head that conditions on the target's bound-state conformation (not just the apo structure), lifting first-try success rate from 18% (v2) to 31%.

On 12 therapeutic targets (PD-1, HER2, EGFRvIII, TNFα, IL-6R, VEGF, c-Met, BCL-2, KRAS-G12C, p53-R175H, Ras, β-amyloid), v3 designs binders with Kd < 10 nM on 9/12 targets. Cryo-EM verifies the design matches the binding pose in 78% of cases (v2 was 71%).

Released as open weights on HuggingFace under CC-BY-NC 4.0. The 8,400-binder library from v3 is downloadable as PDB + paired Kd + cryo-EM structures.

Demo — alphaproteo_v3_design(target_pdb, target_epitope, conformer) - 5-step binder design

5-step pipeline: target parsing, conformer prediction, binder backbone diffusion, sequence design, AF-Multimer filter.

Math → Code derivation

binder = argmax_b P(b | target_conformer) · e^(-λ · ΔG_pred)
AlphaProteo v3 conditions the diffusion on the target's bound conformer, not just the apo structure. The loss combines binding likelihood with a learned ΔG head.
Step 1: Target + epitope
target, epitope = parse_pdb(target_pdb)
Parse the target PDB and identify the binding epitope (the surface patch the binder should target).
Step 2: Conformer prediction
conformer = AlphaFold3.predict_complex(target, generic_binder_template)
Predict the target's bound-state conformer using AlphaFold 3 — not the apo structure. The conformer differs from apo by ~2 Å RMSD, which is crucial for design.
Step 3: Binder backbone diffusion
backbone = diffusion(conformer, epitope, n_steps=250)
Conditional diffusion over binder backbone torsion angles (φ, ψ, ω), 250 denoising steps. The model learns from 4.2M known binder-target pairs.
Step 4: Sequence design
seq = ProteinMPNN(backbone, ss_constraint='stable_fold')
Inverse folding: given the designed backbone, assign a sequence. ProteinMPNN with a secondary-structure constraint to ensure foldability.
Step 5: AF-Multimer filter + Kd head
filter: pLDDT>80 AND pAE(interface)<5 AND Kd_pred<10 nM
Multi-filter: binder must fold (pLDDT>80), interface must be confident (pAE<5), and a learned Kd head predicts Kd < 10 nM. Pass rate ~31% on first try.
