Sora-vision medical: video-input multimodal LLM for clinical video review

OpenAI Health Sciences · OpenAI technical report · 2026-12-15

Abstract

Sora-vision medical is OpenAI's multimodal LLM fine-tuned from Sora 2 for clinical video review. Inputs: endoscopy video, surgical video, echocardiogram loops, ultrasound sweeps. Outputs: timestamped findings, severity grading, recommended next steps.

On the OpenEndo-2026 benchmark (200 endoscopy videos × 5 findings each), Sora-vision medical achieves 0.87 mean average precision (mAP) — beating specialist models (Med-Gemini-Vid 0.74, General Sora 2 0.58). Mean time-to-first-finding: 4.2 seconds (vs 12.5s for a human endoscopist).

Stanford GI pilot (3-month, 4 clinics): flagged 23 polyps that the endoscopist missed in real-time (24% of total polyps found) — likely representing the difference between early-stage detection and progression to cancer.

findings = Sora-med(video); severity = grade(findings); ts = timestamp(findings)
Sora-vision medical processes the video, detects findings, grades severity, and outputs timestamped annotations.
Step 1: Video parse
video = parse_mp4(file); fps = 30
Parse the video MP4. Standardise to 30 fps. Extract audio track (if any) for verbal dictation.
Step 2: Frame sampling
frames = sample(video, stride=3) — every 3rd frame (10 fps effective)
Sample every 3rd frame (10 fps effective) to fit the model's context. Each frame is 224×224 RGB.
Step 3: Multimodal encode
emb = ViT-video(frames) + audio_encoder(audio_track)
A video-ViT (3D attention) encodes the frame sequence. An audio encoder (1D-CNN) encodes the audio track. Embeddings concatenated + cross-attended.
Step 4: Finding detection
findings = detection_head(emb); ts = localise(findings, frames)
A detection head outputs (class, timestamp, confidence) tuples. Mean 5.2 findings per video on OpenEndo-2026.
Step 5: Severity grading + recommendation
severity = grade(findings); next_step = recommend(findings, severity)
Findings are graded (e.g. polyp: 0-IV by Paris classification). Next-step recommendation (e.g. 'refer for polypectomy'). Stanford pilot flagged 23 missed polyps in 3 months.
