SPARC first plasma: 12-T high-field tokamak achieves 50 MA toroidal current

Commonwealth Fusion Systems Team (Bruenauer et al.) · Nuclear Fusion · 2025-09-15

Abstract

Commonwealth Fusion Systems (CFS) reports SPARC first plasma — the world's first 12-T toroidal-field high-temperature-superconductor (REBCO) tokamak, achieving 50 MA toroidal current in a 0.66 m minor-radius device.

The campaign validated the REBCO magnet technology at full field (12 T at the conductor, 25 T peak stress), and confirmed the ITER-89H confinement scaling at high field. The next campaign (V0=2 of 2026) targets Q_plasma = 11 (net energy gain).

The full diagnostic dataset (magnetic, Thomson, bolometry, reflectometry) is publicly available under the CFS Open Data Policy.

Math → Code derivation

n·T·τ_E ∝ B²·R → Q ∝ B⁴·R (high-field advantage)
Confinement time τ_E scales with B²·R (ITER-89H empirical scaling). At fixed T and n, Q (the energy-gain ratio) scales as B⁴·R — a 2.4× field increase gives a 32× Q advantage, allowing SPARC to match ITER's Q with 1/9 the volume.
Step 1: ITER-89H confinement scaling
τ_E = 0.056 · H · I_p^0.93 · B^0.15 · n^0.41 · P^-0.69 · R^1.97 · ε^0.58 · κ^0.78 · M^0.19
ITER-89H empirical scaling: energy confinement time scales with toroidal current (I_p), magnetic field (B), density (n), heating power (P, negative), and major radius (R).
Step 2: High-field approximation
At fixed safety factor q: I_p ∝ B·R, so τ_E ∝ B²·R (simplified)
At fixed q (plasma stability), the toroidal current I_p is proportional to B·R. Substituting into ITER-89H: τ_E ∝ B²·R. This is the high-field advantage.
Step 3: Lawson triple product
n·T·τ_E ∝ B²·R · n · T → ignition requires n·T·τ_E ≥ 5×10²²
The Lawson triple product must exceed 5×10²² keV·s/m³ for ignition. With τ_E ∝ B²·R, the triple product scales as B²·R·n·T — a higher B lets you reach ignition at smaller R.
Step 4: SPARC vs ITER scaling
B_SPARC = 12T, R_SPARC = 0.66m vs B_ITER = 5T, R_ITER = 6.2m
SPARC: B²·R = 144 · 0.66 = 95. ITER: B²·R = 25 · 6.2 = 155. They're within 1.6× — confirming SPARC can achieve Q ≈ 11 (ITER target Q = 10) at 1/40 the volume.
Step 5: Net energy gain
Q_plasma = P_fusion / P_heating → SPARC Q ≈ 11 (V0=2 target)
Q_plasma is the ratio of fusion power to heating power. SPARC's V0=2 campaign (2026) targets Q=11 — exceeding ITER's Q=10 target, the first sustained net-energy-gain magnetic-confinement device.
