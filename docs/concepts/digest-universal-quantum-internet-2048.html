Futuristic Vision (2048): universal quantum internet — global entangled network

AIHPC Editorial Board - extrapolated from Quantum-Heron 5 (2026), QCN (2042), satellite quantum networking (2038) · AIHPC Futuristic Vision · 2048-Q3 (extrapolated)

Abstract

This is a futuristic vision, not a real publication. We extrapolate from IBM Quantum-Heron 5 (2026), the quantum consciousness network (2042), and satellite-based quantum networking (2038) to predict that by Q3 2048, a universal quantum internet will connect every quantum computer, quantum sensor, and quantum-consciousness interface on Earth + in orbit via entangled links.

The vision: a 3-tier quantum network — (1) ground-level fibre links connecting 10,000 quantum computers + 100,000 quantum sensors, (2) LEO-satellite entanglement distribution at 10,000 km range, (3) lunar L2 repeater for intercontinental non-local links. Total: 10^8 entangled nodes. Network latency: 0 (entanglement is non-local).

By 2048, the quantum internet carries 10^12 quantum bits per second of entanglement — enabling: quantum-cloud computing (any quantum computer accessible from any terminal), distributed quantum sensing (10^-15 m resolution gravitational-wave detection), and the quantum-consciousness network (Phase 45 vision) at global scale.

The quantum internet topology: 10,000 ground-level fibre links, 500 LEO satellites for entanglement distribution, 1 lunar L2 repeater for intercontinental non-local links, 10^8 total nodes.
Step 2: Entanglement routing
path = Q-router(A, B, topology, optimise=fidelity × latency)
The quantum router finds the optimal entanglement path between A and B, optimising for fidelity × latency. Since entanglement is non-local, latency ≈ 0 for entangled links.
Step 3: Repeater chain
chain = build_repeaters(path, n_repeaters=distance/100km)
Build a chain of quantum repeaters along the path. Each repeater extends entanglement by ~100 km (fibre) or ~10,000 km (satellite). Repeater fidelity: 0.999 each.
Step 4: End-to-end verification
fidelity = product(chain_fidelities); accept if fidelity > 0.95
End-to-end fidelity = product of all repeater fidelities. For a 10-repeater chain: 0.999^10 = 0.990. Accept if > 0.95. Typical: 0.97-0.99.
