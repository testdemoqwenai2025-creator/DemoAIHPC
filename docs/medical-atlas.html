AI Medical Imaging — recent research + tooling

The atlas highlights the convergence of foundation models + clinical AI. Below the case studies are the open-source tools we used as inspiration:

  • TotalSegmentator (Wass et al. 2023, Radiology) — 117 organs in 6 s/CT, nnU-Net backbone, Apache 2.0.
  • nnU-Net v2 (Isensee et al. 2021, Nature Methods) — self-configuring pipeline; still SOTA backbone.
  • MedSAM (Ma et al. 2024, Nature Communications) — 1.5M-image SAM fine-tune; generalist medical SAM.
  • SAM-Med2D (Cheng et al. 2023) — SAM fine-tuned for 2D medical (X-ray, MRI).
  • BiomedCLIP (Zhang et al. 2024) — CLIP for medical image-text retrieval.
  • RadFM (Wu et al. 2023) — Radiology Foundation Model: 2D + 3D medical LLM.
  • Med-Gemini (Saab et al. 2024, Nature) — Gemini fine-tuned for multimodal medical reasoning.
  • MAISI (NVIDIA 2024) — Latent diffusion for synthetic CT/MRI generation.

Pure client-side — all case-study slices are procedurally generated. For production DICOM parsing, use the Phase 41 scientific-uploader with cornerstone.js (WASM) for full pixel-data decode.
