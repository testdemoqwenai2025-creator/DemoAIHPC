About the Quantum Shorts Channel

The AIHPC platform's quantum visualisation suite spans three depth levels, from single-qubit to many-body quantum mechanics:

  • Single-qubit (Bloch sphere) — 9 animated scenes: ground state, Hadamard, Rabi oscillation, phase gate S, measurement collapse, decoherence, Bell |Φ+⟩, Bell singlet |Ψ-⟩, CHSH violation (2-qubit). Record as 9:16 WebM. Full page →
  • Variational quantum circuits (VQC) — 2-8 qubit parameterised circuits trained via the parameter-shift rule. Shows the live loss landscape + barren-plateau variance chart. QGNN pipeline diagram (classical GNN → angle encoding → VQC → Hamiltonian → measure → train). Full page →
  • Second quantisation (Fock states) — Quantum harmonic oscillator in the Fock basis |n⟩. Animated creation (a†) and annihilation (a) operators showing the √n / √(n+1) coefficients. Wavefunctions via Hermite polynomials. Extends Bloch/VQC into many-body QM. Full page →

All animations are pure client-side canvas + MediaRecorder. Nothing leaves the browser. The QGNN pipeline is an SVG diagram (Phase 39).
