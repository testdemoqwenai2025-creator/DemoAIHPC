Chemistry

Chemistry · atoms & bonds

Bonds → Reactions → Molecules

From atoms to materials. Modern AI for every step.

Modern AI for chemistry — organic, inorganic, physical, analytical, materials, and computational subdomains. Inspired by DeepMind's GNoME (materials discovery), AlphaFold-style molecular structure prediction, and graph-neural-network molecular property models. Each subdomain card below is a dedicated page. 📡 Chemistry feeds →

Subdomain

Organic Chemistry

Reaction prediction, retrosynthesis, SMILES / InChI parsing. Graph neural networks for reaction-class classification and product prediction.

Tools: RDKit (Pyodide), GNN, SMILES parser
Subdomain

Inorganic Chemistry

Crystal-structure prediction, coordination chemistry, ligand-field theory. GNoME-style materials discovery.

Tools: VASP-style DFT (mock), symmetry groups, CIF export
Subdomain

Physical Chemistry

Thermodynamics, kinetics, quantum chemistry. Transition-state search, Arrhenius plots, partition-function calculators.

Tools: ODE solver, TST, Boltzmann sampling
Subdomain

Analytical Chemistry

NMR / MS / IR spectrum processing, peak picking, deconvolution. Chromatogram integration.

Tools: FFT, Gaussian deconvolution, peak picker
Subdomain

Materials Chemistry

Battery materials, catalysts, polymers. Phase-diagram exploration and Pourbaix diagrams.

Tools: Phase-diagram viewer, Pourbaix, convex hull
Subdomain

Computational Chemistry

Molecular dynamics, DFT, semi-empirical methods. Force-field parameterisation and free-energy perturbation.

Tools: OpenMM (Pyodide), xTB, FEP
