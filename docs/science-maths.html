Mathematics

Mathematics · proof & structure

Conjecture → Proof → Theorem → Theory

From intuition to formal truth. Modern AI for every step.

Modern AI for mathematics — algebra, analysis, geometry, number theory, topology, probability & statistics. Inspired by DeepMind's AlphaProof (theorem proving in Lean), AlphaGeometry (synthetic geometry), and AlphaTensor (matrix multiplication algorithms). 📡 Mathematics feeds →

Subdomain

Theorem Proving

AlphaProof-style natural-language to formal proof in Lean 4. Step-by-step tactic trace with verifier acceptance and downloadable proof certificate.

Tools: Lean 4, MCTS, LLM, knowledge graph
Subdomain

Algebra

Group theory, ring theory, Galois theory. Computational algebra with Gröbner bases and polynomial-system solvers.

Tools: Singular (Pyodide), Gröbner, sympy
Subdomain

Analysis

Real and complex analysis, measure theory, functional analysis. Numerical PDE solvers and spectral methods.

Tools: FEM, spectral, FFT
Subdomain

Geometry

AlphaGeometry-style synthetic geometry, differential geometry, computational topology. AlphaTensor for tensor decomposition.

Tools: AlphaGeometry, AlphaTensor, sympy.geometry
Subdomain

Number Theory

Primality testing, elliptic-curve cryptography, analytic number theory. Riemann-zeta zero finder.

Tools: BPSW, ECPP, L-function
Subdomain

Probability & Statistics

Stochastic processes, Bayesian inference, MCMC. BetaBoost / Variational inference in-browser.

Tools: HMC, Stan-style, PyMC (Pyodide)
