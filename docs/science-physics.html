Subdomain

Space & Astrophysics

Orbital mechanics, N-body simulation, exoplanet transit detection. Light-curve analysis from Kepler / TESS-style data.

Tools: N-body solver, FFT, Box-Least-Squares

Waves & Optics

EM spectrum, diffraction, interference. FFT-based spectroscopy, lens-aberration modelling, holography.

Tools: FFT, Fresnel propagator, Zernike polynomials
Subdomain

Sound & Acoustics

Wave equation, room-acoustics simulation, Fourier acoustics. Real-time spectrum and spectrogram visualisation.

Tools: STFT, Web Audio, FDTD solver
Subdomain

Mechanics

Classical mechanics, rigid-body dynamics, Lagrangian / Hamiltonian formalism. Constraint-based simulators.

Tools: ODE solver, Verlet, LCP solver
Subdomain

Quantum

Schrödinger-equation solver, quantum-circuit simulation, Pauli-transfer matrices. Variational quantum eigensolver.

Tools: Sparse linear algebra, VQE, Qiskit (Pyodide)
Subdomain

Thermodynamics

Equations of state, phase equilibria, statistical mechanics. Monte-Carlo simulation of ensembles.

Tools: MCMC, Metropolis, EOS calculators
