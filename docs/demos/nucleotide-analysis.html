Nucleotide Analysis

Paste a DNA sequence and run a battery of analyses entirely in your browser: open-reading-frame finding (six-frame scan), GC-content sliding window, KMP motif search, and Nussinov RNA secondary-structure prediction. Output as FASTA / GenBank Blob. The default sequence is a real published E. coli lacZ gene fragment (NCBI RefSeq NC_000913.3, positions 1451..1550 of lacZ).
