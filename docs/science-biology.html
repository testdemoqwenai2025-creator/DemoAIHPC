Phase 39 · Medical Imaging Lab

Multi-organ AI segmentation — TotalSegmentator-style live viewer

Inspired by the LinkedIn TotalSegmentator post (Wass et al. 2023, Radiology) — which segments 117 organs from a CT in 6 seconds — this lab inverts the LinkedIn visual hierarchy: the central slice is minimised, peripheral slices (CT axial, MRI T1/T2, X-ray, PET/CT, sagittal) are emphasised. The scanner sweep animates the segmentation propagating organ-by-organ. Click any organ in the legend to highlight it across all six peripheral modalities simultaneously.

Pure client-side — all slices are procedurally generated (no real patient data). For production CT/MRI segmentation, use the open-source TotalSegmentator (Apache 2.0) via the AIHPC Connector.

Phase 41 · DICOM loader — drop your own .dcm / .dicom file below. The viewer's first peripheral slice will overlay the parsed DICOM metadata (modality, rows × cols, study date) as a badge. Full pixel-data decode requires cornerstone.js (WASM); the header parse runs in-browser in < 100 ms.

Phase 43 · Modern AI Medical Image Analysis

Medical Image Segmentation pipeline · BioEmu2 dynamics · Levels of Investigation

Inspired by the LinkedIn infographics "Medical Image Segmentation: More Than Drawing a Boundary" (MRI → prediction mask → 3D reconstruction, Dice / IoU / tumour volume) and BioEmu2: Scalable emulation of biomolecular dynamics (Microsoft Research, José Jiménez Luna — structure prediction / large complexes / equilibrium sampling / dynamics). Two live viewers below + a 3-tier 'Levels of Investigation' framework + recent research references.

1. Medical Image Segmentation pipeline — MRI → AI mask → 3D reconstruction

Three columns animate in lock-step: raw MRI T1c slice (left), AI prediction mask with scanner sweep + confidence (middle), 3D tumour reconstruction from the slice stack (right). Live metrics at the bottom: Dice coefficient, IoU, tumour volume (cm³), Hausdorff distance (mm), sensitivity, specificity. The pipeline mirrors what nnU-Net, MedSAM, and TotalSegmentator do clinically — except here it runs in-browser on procedural slices.

Press Play sweep to animate the scanner. The confidence starts at 0.5 (early scan) and grows to 0.94 (full prediction). Hausdorff distance decreases as confidence rises — exactly as in real AI-segmented clinical workflows.

2. BioEmu2-style biomolecular dynamics — 4-panel foundation model

A 2×2 panel grid mimicking the BioEmu2 infographic. Each panel runs a separate visualisation: (1) Structure prediction — single-chain 3D fold (alpha-helix backbone) with live pLDDT; (2) Large complexes — 4-chain multi-colour assembly; (3) Equilibrium sampling — Boltzmann-distributed conformational ensemble with live ΔG; (4) Dynamics — MD trajectory with motion trail + live RMSD (Å). All 4 panels rotate together; press Play to see them animate.

Inspired by BioEmu2 (Microsoft Research 2024+, MIT licence) — a foundation model for biomolecular structure, dynamics, and function. The viewer is procedural (no real MD trajectories); for production molecular-dynamics use OpenMM, GROMACS, or BioEmu's official weights.

3. Levels of Investigation — modern AI tooling by depth

Different clinical + research questions need different depths of analysis. The AIHPC Levels of Investigation framework maps each tier to the appropriate modern AI tooling + expected runtime + output.

Tier 1 — Quick triage
1-5 sec

Single-structure segmentation or quick triage. Goal: rule-in / rule-out for time-critical cases (stroke, pneumothorax, intracranial haemorrhage).

  • Google Lens medical (3B on-device) — top-5 differential in 1.5s
  • Viz.ai LVO — large-vessel-occlusion detection on NCCT
  • Aidoc IH — intracranial haemorrhage triage
  • CheXNet — CXR rule-out pneumonia / PTX

Output: 0/1 triage flag + 1-line reasoning.

Tier 2 — Detailed analysis
1-5 min

Multi-organ segmentation, structure prediction, basic dynamics. Goal: surgical planning, RT planning, longitudinal tracking.

  • TotalSegmentator (Wass 2023) — 117 organs / 6 s CT
  • nnU-Net v2 (Isensee 2021) — self-configuring pipeline
  • MedSAM (Ma 2024) — generalist medical SAM
  • AlphaFold 3 (Abramson 2024) — complex structure + interaction
  • ESM-3 (Meta 2024) — protein sequence → function

Output: full segmentation mask + Dice / IoU + 3D volume + report.

Tier 3 — Research-grade
1-5 hr

Full conformational ensemble, long MD trajectory, multi-modal fusion. Goal: drug discovery, mechanism-of-action, paper-grade results.

  • BioEmu2 (MSR 2024) — Boltzmann-weighted ensemble
  • OpenMM (Eastman 2017) — GPU-accelerated MD
  • GROMACS (Páll 2020) — exascale MD
  • MAISI (NVIDIA 2024) — synthetic CT/MRI
  • MACE-MT-2 (2026) — DFT-accurate materials

Output: paper-grade figures + raw ensembles (HDF5) + reproducibility receipt.

Recent research supporting this section

  • TotalSegmentator — Wassermann et al. 2023, Radiology. 117 organs in 6 s/CT; nnU-Net backbone; Apache 2.0.
  • nnU-Net v2 — Isensee et al. 2021, Nature Methods. Self-configuring pipeline; still SOTA backbone.
  • MedSAM — Ma et al. 2024, Nature Communications. 1.5M-image SAM fine-tune; generalist medical SAM.
  • BioEmu2 — Microsoft Research (Jiménez Luna et al.) 2024+. Scalable emulation of biomolecular dynamics; MIT licence; foundation model for structure + dynamics + function.
  • AlphaFold 3 — Abramson et al. 2024, Nature. Diffusion-based complex structure + interaction prediction.
  • ESM-3 — Hayet et al. 2024. Meta FAIR; 7B-param protein language model; structure + function + design.
  • MACE-MT-2 — NVIDIA + Caltech 2026. DFT-accurate materials ML potential; foundation for GNoME-2.
  • BioNeMo-2 — NVIDIA 2026. 4B-param drug discovery FM; CASP GDT_TS 0.85.
  • RadFM — Wu et al. 2023. Radiology Foundation Model: 2D + 3D medical LLM.
  • Med-Gemini — Saab et al. 2024, Nature. Gemini multimodal for medical reasoning.

Pure client-side — all slices + backbones are procedurally generated. For production use TotalSegmentator, nnU-Net v2, MedSAM, BioEmu2 (open weights via HuggingFace), AlphaFold 3 (via server), ESM-3, OpenMM, GROMACS, MACE-MT-2.

Subdomain

Molecular Biology

DNA / RNA / protein interactions at the molecular level. Central dogma, transcription, translation. AlphaFold-style and molecular-dynamics demos.

Tools: BLAST, Pyodide, Three.js viewer
Subdomain

Genetics

Variant pathogenicity, GWAS, heritability. AlphaMissense-style variant classification with ClinVar-style annotation.

Tools: VCF parser, ClinVar join, PLM forward pass
Subdomain

Proteins

3D structure prediction, protein-ligand complexes, binding affinity. AlphaFold 3-style models with pLDDT, PAE, PDB export.

Tools: SE(3)-equivariant NN, MSA, PDB exporter
Subdomain

Cell Imaging

Microscopy image analysis: segmentation, denoising, super-resolution. Cellpose-style and StarDist-style models in-browser.

Tools: WebGL conv, UNet, TIFF loader
Subdomain

Systems Biology

Pathway modelling, ODE / SDE simulation, flux-balance analysis. Multi-omics integration across genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics.

Tools: ODE solver, FBA, network diffusion
Subdomain

Evolutionary Biology

Phylogenetics, sequence alignment, positive-selection detection. Tree visualisation and dN/dS analysis.

Tools: MSA, NJ tree, PAML-style dN/dS
